RBI LENDING GUIDELINES - MASTER CIRCULAR ON RETAIL LOANS

1. INTRODUCTION
This Master Circular consolidates the instructions and guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to all commercial banks regarding retail and individual lending.

2. LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) RATIOS FOR HOUSING LOANS
Banks must adhere to the following Loan-to-Value (LTV) limits for individual housing loans:
- For loans up to Rs. 30 Lakh: Maximum LTV ratio of 90%.
- For loans between Rs. 30 Lakh and Rs. 75 Lakh: Maximum LTV ratio of 80%.
- For loans above Rs. 75 Lakh: Maximum LTV ratio of 75%.

3. DEBT-TO-INCOME AND AFFORDABILITY
Banks must verify the repayment capacity of the borrower before sanctioning loans. 
- The total monthly debt-service obligations (including the proposed loan EMI) should not exceed 50% to 60% of the borrower's verified net monthly income.
- For borrowers with high-risk profiles (credit score below 650 or default history), the maximum annuity-to-income ratio should be limited to 40%.

4. RESTRUCTURING AND FINANCIAL HEALTH IMPROVEMENT
Borrowers who are facing temporary financial distress may request loan restructuring under the following conditions:
- Extension of loan tenure, up to a maximum of 2 additional years, to reduce monthly EMI.
- Temporary moratorium on principal payments for up to 6 months.
- Borrowers are advised to improve their financial health score by:
  a. Reducing outstanding credit card balances (aiming for utilization below 30%).
  b. Paying off existing high-interest short-term personal loans.
  c. Ensuring no late payments on active loans for at least 6 consecutive months.
