NEUROFINANCE AI - OFFICIAL DOCUMENTATION FOR LOAN APPLICATIONS

LIST OF REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR LOAN SANCTION AND DISBURSEMENT

All applicants must submit self-attested copies of the following documents to the credit risk department for verification:

1. PROOF OF IDENTITY (KYC Documents)
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for e-KYC verification)
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card (mandatory for credit bureau check)
- Passport, Voter ID Card, or Driving License (as additional photo ID proof)

2. PROOF OF ADDRESS
- Utility bills (Electricity, Water, or Gas bill not older than 2 months)
- Aadhaar Card with updated address
- Registered Rent Agreement (if living in rented accommodation)

3. INCOME PROOF (Salaried Applicants)
- Salary slips for the last 3 consecutive months
- Form 16 issued by the employer for the last 2 financial years
- Bank account statement for the last 6 months showing salary credits
- Latest Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2 years

4. INCOME PROOF (Self-Employed / Business Owners)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) along with computation of income for the last 2 financial years
- Audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account for the last 2 years
- Bank account statement of the business/current account for the last 6 months
- Proof of business existence (GST registration certificate, Trade license, or Partnership deed)

5. PROPERTY DOCUMENTS (For Housing Loans)
- Allotment Letter from the builder/authority
- Registered Sale Deed or Agreement to Sell
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the builder/society
- Approved building layout plans
